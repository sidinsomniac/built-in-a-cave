# Choosing a store

- **SQL:** tables, primary and foreign keys, joins, transactions. For relationships, rich queries, and money.
- **Key-value:** lookups by key at huge scale (short links, sessions).
- **Document:** flexible, nested records (profiles).
- **Search index:** text search with typos and ranking.
- **Object storage:** files (photos, video, backups).
- **Time-series:** measurements by time range (metrics, sensors).
- **Choose by:** the read pattern, the consistency needed, and the scale. Real systems use several stores.
